iGaming Responsible Design Accessibility

Two age groups, two opposing UX challenges.

One design revamp, built to responsibly serve both — older players locked out by flash, and younger players pulled in too deep.

Our user base skewed heavily toward older players across European markets, but v1's interface leaned flashy, with fast, decorative animations. That created real accessibility barriers for our core demographic. We challenged the initial design direction and rebuilt around clarity: reduced motion, simplified visual noise, and a monochrome palette that improved legibility and usability without sacrificing brand feel.

Before

Fast motion, dense decoration, low contrast — flashy, but hard to parse.

After

Reduced motion, clear grid, monochrome palette — legible without losing brand.

At the other end of the spectrum, we saw a different risk emerging among younger players — deeper engagement was tipping into compulsive play and increased spend. We addressed this head-on by introducing Reality Check (RC) prompts: contextual pop-ups triggered once a player crossed a defined winnings threshold, nudging them to step away after reaching their daily maximum.
