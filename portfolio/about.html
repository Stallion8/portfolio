I'm Pratik Tamgadge, a UX Lead and QA Engineer with 11+ years of experience, currently working remotely from Pune with Testlio. My client portfolio spans Snapchat, Microsoft (SharePoint/WEX), Bet365, Bajaj Motors, and PlugShare, with earlier roles at Expleo Group and Paperlite.io.

The dual identity isn't a hedge between two career paths — it's the actual differentiator. Designing with a QA instinct means I catch the edge cases before a reviewer has to. Testing with a designer's eye means I flag what's technically correct but still feels wrong to use.
