Paperlite.io iOS Heuristic Audit

Closing the gap between audit and action.

A structured usability audit that reads like a story, not a checklist.

During my time at Paperlite.io, the iOS app carried the accumulated debt of rapid feature growth — inconsistent interaction patterns, buried navigation, and accessibility gaps that were easy to miss without a systematic pass. Rather than filing another scattered bug list, I ran a full heuristic evaluation and surfaced 25 distinct issues, each mapped to severity and a concrete fix.

25
distinct usability & accessibility issues surfaced, each mapped to severity and a fix

The bigger shift was in how the findings were delivered: an interactive report structured like a diagnostic, not a wall of bullet points — so product and engineering could act on priority issues immediately instead of triaging a spreadsheet.

Each issue was logged the way I'd log a QA defect, not just a design note: what's wrong, where it shows up, how severe it is, and what the fix looks like. That structure is what let engineering triage by severity instead of by whoever wrote the loudest bullet point — the same discipline I'd apply to a functional bug report, applied to usability instead.

"A usability audit only matters if someone can act on it. This one was built to be acted on."
