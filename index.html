Two disciplines, one practice.

Most designers hand off to QA. I've spent my career doing both — which means I design with an instinct for edge cases, and I test with an eye for what actually makes an experience feel right. That dual lens has shaped my work across Snapchat, Microsoft (SharePoint/WEX), Bet365, Bajaj Motors, and PlugShare, with earlier roles at Expleo Group and Paperlite.io.

I'm currently a UX Lead and QA Engineer working remotely with Testlio from Pune, India.
